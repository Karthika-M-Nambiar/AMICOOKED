### Project Description
AmICooked? is a completely unnecessary but surprisingly accurate student survival analyzer that calculates how academically "COOKED" you are.

Answer a few brutally honest questions about your studies, deadlines, sleep, phone usage and procrastination, and our highly questionable cooking algorithm decides your academic fate — from RAW to BURNT. 🔥💀

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Students already know they are procrastinating.

They know the exam is tomorrow.  
They know the syllabus isn't finished.  
They know they spent 4 hours on their phone instead of studying.

But somehow, they still need a website to confirm it. 💀

The ridiculous problem we are solving is:

**"How do I know exactly how cooked I am before my exam?"**

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We built AmICooked? — an advanced academic cooking detection system nobody asked for.

The user enters information about their preparation, remaining syllabus, deadlines, phone usage, sleep, procrastination, study time and confidence.

Our completely unnecessary cooking algorithm combines these factors and generates a "Cooked Score" from 0–100.

The result includes an academic temperature, cooking level, Malayalam roasts, chef verdicts, ingredient breakdowns, rescue suggestions and cooking history.

Because apparently students need to know whether they are RAW, CRISPY, DEEP FRIED or completely BURNT. 💀🔥
